Support and contact

Effective date: 28 October 2026

For questions about Réveil, purchases or your data: sav.avisgenius@gmail.com. Publisher: Emile Jouannet, EI (France). Postal and privacy contacts: legal notice and privacy policy.

Managing a subscription?

Open iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or Apple subscription management. Deleting Réveil does not cancel your subscription. To recover access, use “Restore purchases” with the Apple Account used for the purchase. To request a refund, use Apple — Report a Problem; Apple decides the request.

Alarm or challenge issue?

Check Alarm permission in iOS Settings and that your alarm rule is enabled, then run “Test my alarm” in Réveil. If the camera or speech recognition is unavailable, switch to typed calculation. To end a session, open the safety menu and hold Emergency Stop for three seconds; the system Stop remains available. If the app warns that a backup may still interrupt, use “Try again” to retry cancellation.

Helping us understand

Describe the issue, what you expected, the Réveil version, iOS version and iPhone model. You can voluntarily attach the local diagnostic export after reviewing it; no report is sent automatically. It may contain dates, codes and technical identifiers. Avoid first names, voice recordings, camera images, passwords and banking information. No Réveil account is needed.

Age and privacy

If a purchase awaits approval, follow Apple’s parental flow. Tell us if a restriction seems incorrect. To exercise data rights, write to the privacy contact in the policy; deleting the app does not automatically delete providers’ purchase data.
