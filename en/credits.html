Voices and characters

Synthetic voices are generated with AI using Gradium before distribution. Characters are fictional: no real person is imitated and no real phone call is made.

The humour stays light: gentle teasing, without genuine insults. You choose the intensity from the options available for your age.

The app sends no entered first name, camera image, microphone audio or transcript to Gradium. There is no live voice generation from your data.

Icons and components

Apple’s SF Symbols icons are used under Apple’s terms. Réveil uses Apple frameworks for alarms, camera, local recognition and purchases, and RevenueCat for financial reporting when configured.
