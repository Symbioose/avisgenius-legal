Privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Réveil works without an app account or a server operated by its publisher. Alarms and challenges are processed on your iPhone; purchases and financial reporting use Apple and RevenueCat.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is Emile Jouannet, sole trader (entrepreneur individuel, EI), France. Postal address: Rue de Vaugirard, 75015 Paris, France. For any question or request about your data: sav.avisgenius@gmail.com.

2. Data on your iPhone

Your first name and its pronunciation personalise the voices. Alarm times, labels, days, challenges, targets, preferences, sessions, results, streaks and technical logs support operation and local troubleshooting. The subscription preselection is randomly assigned and stored locally, without changing prices or access or being sent to SDKs.

The camera analyses movement for push-ups; the microphone and local speech recognition check challenge answers. Images, audio and transcripts are processed temporarily, without recordings retained or transmission by Réveil. Counters and results may remain in local history.

You can decline or withdraw camera and microphone permissions in iOS Settings. Typed calculation remains available; no server speech recognition takes over. You can change your first name in Réveil settings. The app does not use CloudKit; iOS backups follow your system settings.

We do not request contacts, GPS location, photo library images, identity documents, an exact birth date or payment card details. No first name, image, audio, transcript or challenge answer is sent to RevenueCat or an AI service. Gradium prepares voices before distribution, without app users’ data. Réveil adds no advertising, IDFA, sale of data or cross-service advertising tracking.

3. Purchases and financial reporting

Apple manages payments, purchases, restoration and subscriptions. StoreKit 2 verifies access on your iPhone; RevenueCat controls neither access nor alarms. See App Store & Privacy and Apple’s privacy policy.

When configured, RevenueCat receives accessible purchase history (products, transactions, dates, status) to report on revenue, renewals, cancellations and refunds. It also receives a pseudonymous identifier, IDFV if available, device model, iOS/app/SDK versions, app identifier, languages, App Store country and technical installation and runtime information. The IP address is visible during connections.

This reporting relies on our legitimate interest in tracking revenue and managing the business. It is separate from optional analytics and remains active after you decline or withdraw analytics consent, including for minors. You may exercise your right to object at sav.avisgenius@gmail.com. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and its data processing agreement.

4. Optional analytics

Analytics rely on your consent, are off by default and can be declined or disabled at any time in settings, without affecting purchased features. No network analytics tool is currently connected: TelemetryDeck receives no data. Any future network collection will be explained here before activation. Declined events are not stored for later transmission.

5. Age and protection of minors

Réveil aims for an App Store 13+ rating with light, fictional humour: gentle teasing, no genuine insults and a choice of intensity. The rating displayed on the App Store applies. The app uses Apple’s age assurance (Declared Age Range) when required by the system and applies restrictions and parental consent under Apple’s rules.

Age and consent signals are processed temporarily on the device; only a broad category remains in memory while the app runs. No age range is sent to SDKs or logs. Technical identifiers for ratings, changes and parental requests stay local, with no age or birth date.

Optional analytics are disabled for identified minors and protected unknown-age users in regulated regions. “Brutal” intensity is unavailable to identified users under 16, minors of unspecified age and protected unknown-age users. Some changes require parental approval before access; a purchase awaiting approval does not unlock the subscription. The alternative challenge and emergency stop remain available. Report any incorrect restriction to sav.avisgenius@gmail.com.

6. Purposes and legal bases

  • Performance of a contract: operation, personalisation, subscription, purchase rights and assistance with the requested service.
  • Legitimate interest: financial reporting through RevenueCat, service security and protection of minors through content restrictions.
  • Consent: optional analytics, withdrawable at any time in settings.
  • Legal obligations: accounting and age checks or permissions when required by applicable rules.

7. Recipients and transfers

The publisher accesses information needed for support and reporting. Apple and RevenueCat process the data described above; the email service delivers messages you send us. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process connection data to serve and secure it.

Apple and RevenueCat may process data outside the European Union, particularly in the United States, with safeguards set out in their policies: standard contractual clauses and/or the Data Privacy Framework depending on the provider and processing. Their policies and the RevenueCat agreement linked above explain these safeguards. GitHub describes transfers, safeguards and log retention in its privacy statement. For more information or a copy of the safeguards: sav.avisgenius@gmail.com.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local data: until the app is fully deleted or data is erased using “Erase my data”. Deleting one alarm does not erase its entire history; offloading the app in iOS keeps its data. System backups are managed separately in iOS.
  • Purchase data: according to Apple’s and RevenueCat’s retention periods and criteria in their policies and processing agreement linked above. Deleting the app neither deletes this data nor cancels the subscription.
  • Support emails: up to 3 years after the last exchange.
  • Accounting records: 10 years from the end of the relevant financial year.

9. Support, website and rights

Support receives your email address and message content. Diagnostic export is voluntary and never sent automatically; review it before attaching it. Avoid body images, voice recordings, passwords and banking details.

This website loads no JavaScript, tracker, audience measurement tool or automatic remote resource. GitHub may process IP addresses, dates, requested URLs and browser information. An external link is contacted only when you open it.

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. You may withdraw consent for the future at any time. Write to sav.avisgenius@gmail.com; we will request only what is needed to handle your request. You may complain to the CNIL or your local authority. Changes to processing will be explained here, with renewed consent where needed.
