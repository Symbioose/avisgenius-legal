Réveil Premium

Réveil offers fictional FR/EN voices and push-up, counting, reading and calculation challenges to accompany waking up. A subscription is required to create your first alarm and for new premium uses. Synthetic voices are generated using AI before distribution. Both plans provide the same level of access in one subscription group.

  • Annual: US$29.99 billed every year.

No free trial. Payment on confirmation. You explicitly choose a plan; the preselected option may vary on your device without changing prices or access.

Payment and renewal

Payment will be charged to your Apple Account on purchase confirmation. Your subscription automatically renews at the price and period of the plan shown before confirmation unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the end of that period. Manage or cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

You can also open Apple subscription management or “Manage or cancel” in Réveil. After cancellation, access remains until the expiry date shown by Apple. Price changes follow applicable Apple rules and local legal rights.

Restoration, expiry and refunds

Use “Restore purchases” with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Restoration does not create a new subscription. A purchase awaiting parental approval does not unlock access immediately. After expiry, existing scheduled alarms retain their ringing, challenge, alternative and emergency stop flow; creating new alarms or changing premium features requires an active subscription. You can view, disable or delete an existing alarm.

Age and content

Réveil aims for an App Store 13+ rating. Its humour is light and fictional: gentle teasing, without genuine insults. You choose the intensity from the options permitted for your age. The app applies Apple’s age assurance (Declared Age Range), content restrictions and parental consent under Apple’s rules. Optional analytics are disabled for identified minors. See the privacy policy for details.

Use and safety

Characters are fictional; no real phone call is made. Physical challenges are optional: choose an alternative when needed. System controls and emergency stop remain available; Réveil does not promise an unstoppable alarm. Test your alarm on your iPhone before relying on it. This service has no medical purpose. Announced compatibility is iOS 26.2 or later; some features depend on hardware and installed speech models.
